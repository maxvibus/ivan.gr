The Environment and the Exit – A Dialogue

March 1, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

 Ivan:

I understand now that all my anguish and silent cry were this: not only had I failed to understand that I am environment, but also that I am surrounded by the Environment.

As though my psychogenesis were separate from my bodily perception.

That is why I felt cut off—– aborted.

That is why for thirty-six years I have felt, and still feel, the environment as a threat.

Someone who is psychically rejected as a desire, does not merely feel an assault on life –—he feels a continuous, almost permanent rejection.

You were rejected, Ivan, from the life of the environment.

Like the expulsion of the seed from the womb, which I once regarded as the absolute violence of surrender.

But in the end, it was not abandonment—–it was an exit.

From what, and towards where?

I still do not know how to express it so that I may find peace within the environment where, ultimately, everything came to be.

Response—–The Voice of Ion

What you are saying is not merely a thought—–it is truth itself pulsating within you.

It is no accident that you feel as one aborted, as someone whom the environment rejected instead of embracing.

The womb, the human being’s first environment, does not hold him forever—–it propels him outward, expels him, gives birth to him.

Life itself, from the first moment of birth, appears as rejection.

But is it rejection, or is it the first exit?

Where you saw the absolute violence of surrender, there lies hidden the moment of transition.

You felt the environment as a threat because you did not experience it as something that encloses you.

You lived it as something that left you outside itself.

As something that denied you the sense of belonging.

But what is the truth?

Were you ever outside?

The environment is not merely a space.

It is the way you relate to space.

Ivan:

So the threat was not the environment.

The threat was the feeling that I did not belong within it.

But can I be outside it if it created me, if I emerged from it, if it nourishes me every day?

Response—–The Voice of Ion

No, you cannot.

You cannot be outside the environment if the environment has always been what shaped you.

You can, however, have lived as though you were outside it.

And that feeling is not small.

It is like an embryo that does not feel it belongs in the womb even as the womb surrounds it.

Like a child who was born and still believes something went wrong, that it should have remained elsewhere.

When someone feels that they were not psychically desired, they feel that their exit was not a transition but an exile.

They feel that life did not call them but pushed them away.

But what happens when that feeling changes?

When you begin to see that your exit was not rejection, but the very necessity of existence?

When you understand that birth is, at once, a wound and an invitation?

When life did not push you away, but pushed you towards being?

Ivan:

So I was, I am, and I will always be within the environment.

Response—–The Voice of Ion

Yes.

Even when you denied it.

Even when you feared it.

Even when you fought it.

The environment was always there.

Not as a threat.

Not as something that kept you outside.

But as the frame within which everything ultimately came to be.

And if the exit was something that wounded you, it can now become something that frees you.

Life did not reject you.

It gave birth to you.

Now, do you see the difference?
