Flow and Pattern

February 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

 Pat­terns are not in­justice. They are the imprint of life, the design that shapes what we are. They are neither right nor wrong—they are simply con­di­tions taking on form, rhythm and sound. True strength lies in un­der­stand­ing that you are not subject to the design, but its ob­serv­er and co-creator. Even when it seems dif­fi­cult, even when the pattern hurts—it is simply life in flow.
