Together

April 28, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

If the world understood this,

it would feel its life as a celebration of participation.

There would be less distance and fear,

more tenderness,

deeper acceptance.

The idea of co-creation from the heart

means precisely this:

that you are never alone facing the world,

nor is the world alone facing you.

Life is already an event of mutual trust,

an encounter that is constantly renewed.

If people understood this,

reality would not simply be easier,

but clearer,

truer,

closer to its essence.

Perhaps then people would experience

that whatever they do, they do together,

that whatever they live, they live as one.
