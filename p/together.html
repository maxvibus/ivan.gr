Together

April 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

If the world un­der­stood this,

it would feel its life as a cel­eb­ra­tion of par­ti­cip­a­tion.

There would be less dis­tance and fear,

more ten­der­ness,

deeper ac­cept­ance.

The idea of co-cre­ation from the heart

means pre­cisely this:

that you are never alone facing the world,

nor is the world alone facing you.

Life is already an event of mutual trust,

an en­counter that is con­stantly renewed.

If people un­der­stood this,

reality would not simply be easier,

but clearer,

truer,

closer to its essence.

Perhaps then people would ex­per­i­ence

that whatever they do, they do to­geth­er,

that whatever they live, they live as one.
