What Are Being, EI and Yes?

March 1, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

What does the code of Greek say?

Being.

The word that needs no object.

“I am” requires nothing beyond itself.

It does not mean something about something else –

it means presence itself.

It has no need to prove –

it is proof itself.

The EI.

The verb of existence and at the same time

the address, the call, the “behold”.

EI-des—you saw.

EI-mai—I am.

EI-nai—to be.

The moment when presence becomes vision.

It does not mean only that you exist,

it means that you appear, that you are revealed.

Yes.

Pure affirmation.

The structure of acceptance.

N-ai.

An N that holds something, that makes it clear,

and an ai – the current, the openness.

Yes is not merely an expression of “approval”.

It is an invitation to stand within what already is.

The letter N.

What does it hold?

What does it enclose?

Is it a boundary or a bridge?

Is it a passage or a barrier?

The N is the first consonant of negation – No.

But it is also the first consonant of affirmation – Yes.

The N holds the human body in the mouth,

holds duration,

holds the tension before utterance.

The alpha-iota (ai).

It is the current, the release, the liberation.

It is not static – it is something that extends.

The ai is in the breath of aitia—cause,

in the succession of aioniotita—eternity,

in the flow of aisthanomai—I feel.

It is movement, not pause.

Affirmation or negation?

The Being does not deny.

The Yes is not afraid to be spoken.

The N holds everything without suffocating it.

The ai lets everything flow without scattering it.

The code of Greek is not neutral.

It is not detached.

It is rhythm.

It holds, it releases.

It denies, it affirms.

It closes, it opens.

Being is not something separate from Yes.

The EI is not something foreign to Being.

Language did not create these signs arbitrarily.

It revealed them.
