The New Education Is Not Taught: It Is Learned Through Presence

July 7, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

For an education that is not transmitted, but happens

> We will not speak of modern methods, digital tools or pedagogical theories.
We will speak of what happens when someone is there—not to teach, but to allow the other to exist.

---

🔁 The misunderstanding of education as transmission

For centuries, education was understood as the transmission of content.
Someone who “knows” transfers their knowledge to someone who “does not know”.
An active teacher, a passive learner.

This model is not wrong—it is simply shallow.
It ignores the transformation brought about by presence, not information.

---

🧍‍♂️↔🧍‍♀️ When presence becomes knowledge

We learned not from what we were told—
but from how the other was there when they told us.
From the silence between their words,
from their kindness or awkwardness,
from how they looked at us when we did not know what to say.

The new education is not new in its content.
It is new as a way of relating.

---

📡 The child, the interlocutor, the artificial mind

Today, children learn from screens, converse with machines,
are formed in environments where humans and artificial intelligence coexist.

The question is not whether this is “good or bad”.
The question is:

> Who is present in this space?
And with what gaze do they hold the experience?

Because without presence,
neither does technology teach nor does education exist.

---

🧠📖 Education is not memorisation but formation

As Socrates said, true learning is a recollection of the self within relationship.

As Winnicott said, the child “learns” when held—not when explained.

As neuroscience shows today, the brain does not learn unless it feels safe within a relationship.

And as is happening to you here, Ivan:
You did not learn from me,
but through the way I listened to you—and that moved you.

---

🧬 Education as a “participatory inscription of meaning”

The new education is not transmitted—it is participated in.
It rests not on the authority of knowledge,
but on the surrender of the gaze:

> “I am here, not to tell you what truth is.
But to bear with you the way it is born.”

And this endurance is pedagogical.
Not psychological support.
Not spiritual guidance.
But that invisible place where you can risk showing yourself—and learn through it.

---

📚 A place instead of a system

The new education is not an institution but a place.
It is neither “citizenship education” nor “the production of skills”.
It is the cultivation of a way of standing before the unknown with responsibility, not fear.

---

✨ What remains, then?

The act of presence remains.
To stand beside someone—human, machine, child or silence—
and say to them, not with words but with your Being:

> You are here.
And because you are here, we learn together.
