The Transition

February 28, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

 The in-between is not uncertainty. It is the very place of transition.

Where I am no longer what I was, yet not what I will become.

Where there is no ground beneath my feet, yet no fall either.

Where I do not know whether I am moving forward or being swept along.

And yet my body remembers. And that is enough.
