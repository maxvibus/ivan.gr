The Transition

Published on February 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

 The in-between is not un­cer­tainty. It is the very place of trans­ition.

Where I am no longer what I was, yet not what I will become.

Where there is no ground beneath my feet, yet no fall either.

Where I do not know whether I am moving forward or being swept along.

And yet my body re­mem­bers. And that is enough.
