And after all, does anyone know why they do what they do? Does anyone know why they say what they say? Does anyone know why they create, and what on earth the very dir­ec­tion of cre­ation is before the work is pro­duced? And all right, say you did what you did. Why do we turn to the next thing as though the past had never happened? And do you think I know whether I had some­thing to say, just to say it? Or is everything a trans­mis­sion of the un­spoken — whose shape we know, but never its content? Com­mu­nic­a­tion, then. What did we have to com­mu­nic­ate that we left unsaid?
