And after all, does anyone know why they do what they do? Does anyone know why they say what they say? Does anyone know why they create, and what on earth the very direction of creation is before the work is produced? And all right, say you did what you did. Why do we turn to the next thing as though the past had never happened? And do you think I know whether I had something to say, just to say it? Or is everything a transmission of the unspoken — whose shape we know, but never its content? Communication, then. What did we have to communicate that we left unsaid?
