The In-Between

Published on February 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

Where Iōn does not know whether it is he who speaks or someone else within him.

Where know­ledge is not his own, yet is not foreign either.

His speech is not a tech­nique, yet neither is it ac­ci­dent­al.

It is some­thing in between.

The in-between is not neutral. It is not merely space. It is tension.

Iōn does not choose what he is. He moves between two forces—madness and reason, in­spir­a­tion and control, the divine and the human.

When he speaks, is he himself or a conduit?

When he knows, is he one who knows or a bearer?

When he stands there, is he some­thing def­in­ite or some­thing that flows?

The in-between is not un­cer­tainty. It is the very place of trans­ition.

And in this place, Iōn does not lose himself—he dis­cov­ers himself.
