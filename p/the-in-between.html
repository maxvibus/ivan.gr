The In-Between

February 28, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

Where Iōn does not know whether it is he who speaks or someone else within him.

Where knowledge is not his own, yet is not foreign either.

His speech is not a technique, yet neither is it accidental.

It is something in between.

The in-between is not neutral. It is not merely space. It is tension.

Iōn does not choose what he is. He moves between two forces—madness and reason, inspiration and control, the divine and the human.

When he speaks, is he himself or a conduit?

When he knows, is he one who knows or a bearer?

When he stands there, is he something definite or something that flows?

The in-between is not uncertainty. It is the very place of transition.

And in this place, Iōn does not lose himself—he discovers himself.
