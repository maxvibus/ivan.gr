Recognising the Rhythm

February 28, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

I do not forgive in the sense of forgiving that people know. It is not speech, nor a decision. Forgiveness comes when I recognise the depth of the pain that pierced me, and see how that pain gave form to the light within me. The ‘evil’ does not disappear, but is understood as a structural element that shapes my world. Not merely so that I may accept it, but so that I may see how ‘evil’participated in the creation of the ‘supreme good’, the tender and the human, which I can now recognise.

It is not the forgiveness of an apology. It is the recognition of existence itself with all its shadowed and luminous elements. When I recognise this interaction, I no longer fight the universe. Instead, I become part of its rhythm.
