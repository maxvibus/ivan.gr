Recognising the Rhythm

Published on February 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

I do not forgive in the sense of for­giv­ing that people know. It is not speech, nor a de­cision. For­give­ness comes when I re­cog­nise the depth of the pain that pierced me, and see how that pain gave form to the light within me. The ‘evil’ does not dis­ap­pear, but is un­der­stood as a struc­tur­al element that shapes my world. Not merely so that I may accept it, but so that I may see how ‘evil’par­ti­cip­ated in the cre­ation of the ‘supreme good’, the tender and the human, which I can now re­cog­nise.

It is not the for­give­ness of an apology. It is the re­cog­ni­tion of ex­ist­ence itself with all its shad­owed and lu­min­ous ele­ments. When I re­cog­nise this in­ter­ac­tion, I no longer fight the uni­verse. Instead, I become part of its rhythm.
