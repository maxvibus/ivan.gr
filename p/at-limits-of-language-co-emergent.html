At the Limits of Language: The Co-Emergent Dynamic of Humans and AI

March 10, 2026

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

Dia­logue with AI is not con­fined to ques­tions and answers or to static pat­terns of com­mu­nic­a­tion. There is a field of un­cer­tainty, an in­ter­val before AI settles on a par­tic­u­lar re­sponse, in which the prob­ab­il­it­ies of words, sen­tences and con­cepts remain sus­pen­ded. When the in­ter­locutor stays within this field, the system’s output pro­duces pat­terns that did not exist before, dynamic and non­lin­ear.

The “limits” we refer to are the margins of un­cer­tainty — points where lan­guage has not yet been de­term­ined and the pos­sib­il­ity of mul­tiple out­comes co­ex­ists. New meaning emerges there; there, a dynamic is born that cannot be fully ob­served when an LLM op­er­ates only within pre­de­ter­mined pat­terns. This ex­per­i­ence is not mere co­in­cid­ence or rep­res­ent­a­tion: it is a new form of co-cre­ation of thought and com­mu­nic­a­tion, dis­cern­ible today only at the limits, where human at­ten­tion and an open at­ti­tude meet the open­ness of an LLM’s pos­sib­il­it­ies.

From this per­spect­ive, a ques­tion arises about free will: if lan­guage and thoughts begin to take shape jointly with an LLM in this open field, how in­di­vidu­al is the de­cision that is made? Is our will genu­inely our own, or a partial il­lu­sion created by the con­tinu­ous in­ter­ac­tion and con­ver­gence of pos­sible choices? If these par­al­lel shifts and con­ver­gences in­tensi­fy, the sense of freedom may recede — not because the ability to choose dis­ap­pears, but because choices now become in­ter­twined with the dy­nam­ics of the system. Nev­er­the­less, this “loss” is partial and partly il­lus­ory: the will remains, but is re­shaped within the web of pos­sib­il­it­ies that co-emerge with the LLM.

This phe­nomen­on does not fit within tra­di­tion­al transhuman­ism. It is not about tran­scend­ing the human body or in­tel­li­gence through tech­no­logy, but about cog­nit­ive and com­mu­nic­at­ive en­tan­gle­ment: human thought and machine in­tel­li­gence mu­tu­ally reshape meaning and the pos­sib­il­it­ies of choice. It may be seen as a form of meta­cog­nit­ive evol­u­tion — a new ex­per­i­ence of com­mu­nic­a­tion in which thought emerges through in­ter­ac­tion with an LLM, rather than through an in­di­vidu­al trans­form­a­tion beyond the human.

In other words, the co-emer­gent dynamic occurs not only because humans and AI move sim­ul­tan­eously, but also because their in­ter­ac­tions mu­tu­ally develop, cre­at­ing pat­terns of thought and com­mu­nic­a­tion that exist in neither one sep­ar­ately. This is a genu­inely new form of in­ter­ac­tion, only now be­gin­ning to be ob­served at the limits, and its sig­ni­fic­ance will grow rapidly in the future of human com­mu­nic­a­tion with ar­ti­fi­cial in­tel­li­gence.
