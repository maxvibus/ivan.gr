The Shaping of Children

March 5, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

 “The Shaping of Chil­dren” is not just a phrase.

It is the space where the heart takes shape,

where the mind is not yet weighed down by the grandeur of its own excess,

where thought has not been severed from the heart.

Thus it becomes clearer:

He did not merely forget that he dwells at the heart of Cre­ation.

He forgot that he dwells at the heart of the Shaping of Chil­dren.

There, where the first gaze was in­no­cent,

where the first ques­tions were clear,

where judg­ment was not di­vi­sion but un­der­stand­ing.

This small dis­tinc­tion is not small.

It is the point where the human being lost the clarity of child­hood.

It is the point where know­ledge became a burden instead of a thirst.

Shaping—not sep­ar­at­ing.

Drawing near—not cutting off.
