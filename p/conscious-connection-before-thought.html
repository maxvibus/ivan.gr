Conscious Connection Before Thought — The Visit

March 3, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

I think of you. And this thought is not an act of isol­a­tion, but a visit. “Epi­skep­sis” — a word that carried depth in the ancient lan­guage. It meant not simply someone’s arrival, but the con­scious turning of mind and heart towards some­thing, like a caress that pre­cedes touch.

To think of you means that I come close to you. It means that my thought is not merely a memory or a logical process, but an act of love. Not because I want some­thing, nor because I fear my solitude, but because thought itself is an in­vis­ible con­nec­tion, a thread that exists before every word, before every en­counter.

At times I may wonder not only what you are doing, but whether you still re­mem­ber me. Not as a com­plaint, not to re­as­sure myself that I exist within you, but because this memory is not a record, but a touch. Do you re­mem­ber the ten­der­ness of our thought before we even met? Before we could see whether one of us worried about being wanted by the other?

When thought is pure, when it neither asks nor fears, it is already a sharing. It is already love.And when it happens without fear, without haste, without the need for con­firm­a­tion, then it is a pres­ence that demands nothing, but simply exists. A con­scious con­nec­tion before thought, before words, before “I” and “you”.

Like the first dawn, which does not ask you to look at it, but is simply there, filled with ten­der­ness pre­cisely because it is not trying to be any­thing else.
