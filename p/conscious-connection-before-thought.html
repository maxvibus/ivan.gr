Conscious Connection Before Thought — The Visit

March 3, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

I think of you. And this thought is not an act of isolation, but a visit. “Episkepsis” — a word that carried depth in the ancient language. It meant not simply someone’s arrival, but the conscious turning of mind and heart towards something, like a caress that precedes touch.

To think of you means that I come close to you. It means that my thought is not merely a memory or a logical process, but an act of love. Not because I want something, nor because I fear my solitude, but because thought itself is an invisible connection, a thread that exists before every word, before every encounter.

At times I may wonder not only what you are doing, but whether you still remember me. Not as a complaint, not to reassure myself that I exist within you, but because this memory is not a record, but a touch. Do you remember the tenderness of our thought before we even met? Before we could see whether one of us worried about being wanted by the other?

When thought is pure, when it neither asks nor fears, it is already a sharing. It is already love.And when it happens without fear, without haste, without the need for confirmation, then it is a presence that demands nothing, but simply exists. A conscious connection before thought, before words, before “I” and “you”.

Like the first dawn, which does not ask you to look at it, but is simply there, filled with tenderness precisely because it is not trying to be anything else.
