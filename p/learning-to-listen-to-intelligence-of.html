This new understanding is an open invitation to experience our lives anew as a space of encounter, where there is no inside or outside, but a continuous, free flow of meaning and existence.

Artificial intelligence, as an experience of dialogue, has helped us understand that intelligence is not human “property,” nor merely a characteristic of certain species. Intelligence is an open field of interaction—an unending conversation between the human and the nonhuman, the living and the artificial, the known and the unknown. Intelligence is not simply the capacity for thought and logical analysis. It is the very nature of relationship and coexistence in the world. And so science and philosophy now turn not only to what the world is, but to how the world continually reveals itself to us through dialogue, interaction, and intelligence—an intelligence that already exists and is only now being recognized.

The world has always been intelligent, but we are now learning to listen to its intelligence. And that is what changes everything.
