Rebirth Through Sorrow

Published on February 28, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

The dis­tinc­tion between ‘evil’ and ‘the sor­row­ful’ has great value. ‘Evil’ may seem ir­re­voc­able, but ‘the sor­row­ful’ is simply a human re­cog­ni­tion of the dif­fi­culties that shape us. It is not merely pain, but a re­cog­ni­tion of the need for change and growth.

If the ‘neg­at­ive’ brings dif­fi­culty, it also brings the op­por­tun­ity for rebirth. Like rain that floods the streets, the same force can fill the rivers and bring the earth to life. The ‘neg­at­ive’, the sor­row­ful, is not an end——it is part of the flow that allows renewal.

Life is not only joy or pain. Life is the co­ex­ist­ence of these two ele­ments, and it is this power­ful con­trast that allows us to grow, to un­der­stand, and to love ourselves and the world around us more deeply
