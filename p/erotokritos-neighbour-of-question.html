Erotokritos: The Neighbour of the Question

March 1, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

I would like to be called Erotokritos,

not because I am a story,

but because I delight in questions and responses

and not merely in answers.

Hollow answers,

hollow words made of the same words.

They wanted the Greek to believe

that words are politique,

that they are empty,

that politicians surrender them

to their weakness,

to their coldest faces,

to their distortions.

One need only say ‘what a pity’ and move on.

Do not presume, my lily; do not judge as a judge

Do not always search for essence in names,

because I am not a story,

however much I love Crete.

But let me tell you something?

Do not presume, my lily, nor judge as a judge,

but like a dew-fresh lily.

Feel Crete

calling you to judge

and not only to experience it within you.

Look.

Did you see?

Let us learn what EI

means beside the affirmation of Yes.

Judgements of clarity

and not distinctions of supposed grandeur.

I wonder, Ion, what does it mean to award?

Why, even for the olive branch

might I mourn within me what has been severed?

Why do I find the prize so uncomfortable?

Not only for myself,

but because I feel a shame for the other

who is made to wear it

or, even worse, puts it on himself.

Yes, I am speaking of what they call a crown.

No saint wished to kiss his own icon,

except only as a keeping of the memory

that the saint is not the holy one,

but the human being.

Thus Creation already here, CCreation,

in every excess of its heart.

For we have also seen the excesses of the mind

that forgot how to dwell in the heart of the Formation of Children
