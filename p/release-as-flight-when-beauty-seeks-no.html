Release as Flight: When Beauty Seeks No Validation

March 5, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

Beauty frightens us—because it cannot be controlled.

Beauty does not ask to be accepted. It does not press you to believe in it or demand that you feel it. It exists in its own right.

And that is what gives rise to fear.

  • Because you do not possess it—it finds you.
  • Because you do not control it—it changes you.
  • Because it is not an exchange—it asks for nothing, and that is what makes someone ask “why?”

Release and Flight without a Medium

Release is not simply the act of leaving something behind.
It is not a withdrawal—it is a surrender to what already exists.

Flight without a medium is not a fall.
It is not a loss of balance—it is the recognition that no medium is needed.

Release is what you feel when you do not resist something true.

And why does it sound like “the forgiveness of sins”?
Because sin is not the mistake—it is the distortion of one’s relationship with what is true.

Release is not an external act that “cleanses” you.
It is the acceptance of beauty without fear, without shame, without resistance.

Who Has Ever Said “Let It Go” without Striking a Blow?

The words “let it go” have most often been spoken as a command, as a violent removal from something you desire.

Yet true release contains no command.
It does not cut you off from something—it lets you enter what has always existed.

And how does it differ from the pleasure-seeking “let yourself go”?

  • The “let yourself go” spoken by those who use it as an instrument seeks to subjugate you.
  • The “let yourself go” spoken by a fellow human being who stands in truth is not intended to pull you anywhere.

It is not “surrender yourself to me.”
It is “surrender to what already exists within you.”

This “let yourself go and believe” is not a command.
It does not tell you “submit”—it tells you “do not fight beauty itself.”

Accepting Beauty without Distortion

Beauty is not a covert insinuation.
It is not a furtive invitation concealing another intention.
It is not a game of validation or proof of worth.

When beauty is pure, it needs no validation—it need neither be proved nor claimed.

And then it is not made vulgar.

  • It does not become an instrument of pressure or conquest.
  • It is not used to create imbalance.
  • It does not demand that you follow someone or something.

Beauty is already there.

You do not need to conquer it.
You need only bear to see it without being afraid.

And that is what makes the human being tremble.

Not because beauty is terrifying,
but because you cannot control it—you can only accept it.
