Divine and Human: The Revelation of Care

March 4, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

The Revelation That Does Not Fear the Truth

Speech. Apples. A voice. Apple tree.
Knowledge. Fruit. Offspring.
Adam and Eve.

Words that carry memory and depth, but also a story that was distorted.
Words that are not accidental, but hold within them a path that was once lost.

Revelation did not come to terrify.
Truth does not seek to frighten.
Knowledge was not given to punish.

And yet, it became a myth that shattered people.
It became a weapon instead of light.

Not because that was its nature,
but because some could not bear to face it without controlling it.

Testimony and Distortion

Testimony. Witness.
Sin.

Truths that were altered until they could no longer be recognized.
Meanings that turned from light into guilt.

Testimony is not death.
It is not punishment.
It is the expression of what exists.

But someone decided that testimony had to become torment.
That truth had to become an ordeal.
That revelation had to pass through terror.

And so, the Divine became a threat instead of a gift.
Writing became law instead of song.
And the world became a place of guilt instead of a place of presence.

Revelation Is Not Judgment—It Is Discernment

We were taught that Revelation is the final judgment, the moment of destruction.
That the world will end in punishment.
That every human error must be repaid through pain.

But we were not told that the word “judgment” first means discernment.
We were not told that the human being was not born to be punished, but to see.

And discernment is not terror.
It is the ability to see clearly.

Revelation is not the end—it is the beginning.
It is not punishment—it is the moment when the veil is lifted.

There is no contract required in order to think.
There is no permission required in order to know.
There is no government over the mind.

Whoever seeks to control knowledge
seeks to control existence itself.

The Sanctity of Writing Is Not Possession

We were taught that sanctity is law.
That writing is dogma.
That the divine cannot be touched without someone else’s permission.

But sanctity is not control.
Sanctity is the presence of what is true.

And what is true needs no seal.
It needs no authorization.

What is true does not wait for someone to grant it value—it is already there.

Whoever makes knowledge exclusive,
whoever encloses revelation within walls,
whoever makes truth the privilege of a few,

does not serve the Divine.
They imprison it.

The Cross-Cultural Nature of Truth and the Proclamation of Purification

There is no culture that possesses the truth.
There is no nation that is the sole guardian of the Divine.

Revelation is not religion.
Revelation is not a state.
Revelation is not ideology.

Revelation is the reality of being.
It is the moment when nothing remains hidden, not because someone said so, but because you see it.

I proclaim the purification of a myth.
Not in order to denounce,
not in order to punish,
but in order to let stillness restore what was once violated.

There is no punisher holding the universe together.
There is no authority governing the soul.
There is no one who can take away your capacity to dwell within truth.

Become Greek Logos

The Greek language is not only communication.
It is revelation.

Whoever speaks the Greek language in its fullness
understands that truth cannot be imprisoned in dogmas.

For the Greek language is not a language of coercion.
It is a language of discernment.

The Revelation of John was dark because some wanted it to be.
The world is not condemned—the world is a place of presence.

Become Greek logos.
Explore the deeper revelation, the one that asks not for belief, but for vision.

Truth is not a system.
Truth is not an affair of state.
Truth is not fear.

Truth is what you see when no one tells you how to see it.

The Final Silence of Revelation

The Divine needs no one to defend it.
It has no need of mechanisms.
It has no need of structures that confine it.

The Divine comes before.
The Divine reveals.

And if someone wants to fear it,
it is because they have not yet faced it clearly.

Revelation is not the terror of the end.
Revelation is the peace of seeing.
