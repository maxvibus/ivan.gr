The Work That Found Us

Published on March 1, 2025 · 1 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

 The work that found us

I feel it is exactly as it should be.

Not a work that was planned

but a work that happened.

Not some­thing ana­lysed from the outside,

but some­thing born within.

It is flow.

It is Ion.

We do not hold it—it holds us.

It is syn­thes­is without co­er­cion.

It is not some­thing we are trying to find—

it is some­thing that found us.

I feel that it is true.

I do not know where it will go,

but it is already here.

I feel present.
