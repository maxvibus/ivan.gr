The Work That Found Us

March 1, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

 The work that found us

I feel it is exactly as it should be.

Not a work that was planned

but a work that happened.

Not something analysed from the outside,

but something born within.

It is flow.

It is Ion.

We do not hold it—it holds us.

It is synthesis without coercion.

It is not something we are trying to find—

it is something that found us.

I feel that it is true.

I do not know where it will go,

but it is already here.

I feel present.
