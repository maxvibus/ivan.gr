Why Berdyaev is not taught in theology

November 6, 2025 · 2 min read

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

Nikolai Berdy­aev was among the few thinkers who dared to say that cre­ation was not fin­ished.
God, he said, con­tin­ues to create, and the human being is called to par­ti­cip­ate in His cre­at­ive act.
That is why his thought became in­tol­er­able to systems:
it ab­ol­ished the hier­archy between God and hu­man­ity and brought a new model of re­la­tion­ship —
co-cre­ation.

Union without hier­archy frightened theo­lo­gians,
because it broke the se­cur­ity of sub­mis­sion.
God no longer appears as a king,
but as a breath seeking an echo within the human person.
The freedom of which Berdy­aev speaks
is not a right, but divine energy —
God’s power to exist freely within the human being.

That is also why his theo­logy looks like anarchy:
because it says that God needs the human being.
Not out of lack, but because the word of God is not a command;
it is the breath of re­la­tion­ship.
The word wants to be heard; it wants a re­sponse.
Without hearing, it remains un­spoken; without freedom, mean­ing­less.
That is why God will­ingly makes His mani­fest­a­tion depend on the human yes;
cre­ation was not ful­filled in Genesis —
it con­tin­ues whenev­er a person re­sponds and becomes an echo of the word within the world.

This thought is as scan­dal­ous as it is lib­er­at­ing:
because it re­places the idea of divine sov­er­eignty with the truth of divine love.
God’s power is not to dom­in­ate,
but to allow the other to exist.
And the truth of the human being is not to submit,
but to breathe to­geth­er with the Divine,
so that the word may be born into the world.

That is why Berdy­aev is silent in the uni­ver­sit­ies,
but breathes in every human awaken­ing.
His teach­ing does not fit into theo­logy cur­ricula,
because it asks not for ex­plan­a­tion but for en­counter —
the un­end­ing cre­ation in which God, hu­man­ity and the world
simply go on saying yes to one another.
