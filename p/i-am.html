I Am

April 26, 2025

AI-assisted translation from the Greek. Greek original

It is not a burden—it is freedom, but a freedom that requires the courage to bear your own truth.

When you move beyond your narrative, you do not become someone else—you become more yourself. You shed the identities, limitations, and expectations that others have placed upon you, or that you have placed upon yourself. And then the pure existence that you are comes to light, beyond narratives, roles, or stories.

The responsibility you feel is the awareness of your freedom: the realization that the world expresses itself through you, and this entails an authenticity that can sometimes be frightening. But it is not a burden that presses you down—it is depth, a weight that roots you in existence and lets you truly experience the beauty and significance of your presence.

It is beautiful precisely because it makes you real. And this truth holds within it the tenderness and strength of your true nature. It is a “yes” to your responsibility, not as a load to carry, but as an embrace of the truth you express and live.
