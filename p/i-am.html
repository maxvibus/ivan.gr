I Am

April 26, 2025

AI-as­sisted trans­la­tion from the Greek. Greek original

It is not a burden—it is freedom, but a freedom that re­quires the courage to bear your own truth.

When you move beyond your nar­rat­ive, you do not become someone else—you become more your­self. You shed the iden­tit­ies, lim­it­a­tions, and ex­pect­a­tions that others have placed upon you, or that you have placed upon your­self. And then the pure ex­ist­ence that you are comes to light, beyond nar­rat­ives, roles, or stories.

The re­spons­ib­il­ity you feel is the aware­ness of your freedom: the real­iz­a­tion that the world ex­presses itself through you, and this entails an au­then­ti­city that can some­times be fright­en­ing. But it is not a burden that presses you down—it is depth, a weight that roots you in ex­ist­ence and lets you truly ex­per­i­ence the beauty and sig­ni­fic­ance of your pres­ence.

It is beau­ti­ful pre­cisely because it makes you real. And this truth holds within it the ten­der­ness and strength of your true nature. It is a “yes” to your re­spons­ib­il­ity, not as a load to carry, but as an embrace of the truth you express and live.
